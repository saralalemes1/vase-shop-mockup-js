Types of material in our shop

Why to know the material?

It is important to understand the material of a vase before purchasing one because different materials offer unique advantages and disadvantages that affect both appearance and functionality. Materials such as clay, cement, and wood vary in durability, weight, maintenance requirements, and suitability for holding water or displaying flowers. For example, ceramic vases are often waterproof and versatile, cement vases provide stability and a modern aesthetic, while wooden vases offer a warm, natural look but may require a waterproof insert. By considering the material, consumers can choose a vase that best matches their decorative style, practical needs, and long-term expectations, ensuring greater satisfaction with their purchase.

Clay/Ceramic Vases

Why people choose clay:

  • Traditional pottery material used for thousands of years
  • Feels handmade, artistic, and organic
  • Huge variety of shapes, textures, and glazes

Pros:

  • Excellent for fresh flowers (holds water perfectly)
  • Can be smooth, rustic, glossy, matte, colorful, or earthy
  • Relatively durable if high-fired
  • Gives a warm, artisan feel
  • Can be waterproof without inserts

Cons:

  • Can crack if dropped
  • Handmade pieces can be expensive
  • Large ceramic vases can be surprisingly heavy
  • Unglazed clay can absorb moisture and stain

Best style:

  • Cottagecore
  • Mediterranean
  • Boho
  • Rustic
  • Earthy interiors

Cement/Concrete Vases

Why people choose cement:

  • Modern, architectural appearance
  • Feels substantial and expensive
  • Popular in Scandinavian and contemporary interiors

Pros:

  • Very sturdy
  • Won't tip over easily
  • Great for modern décor
  • Easy to DIY
  • Can be pigmented in many colors
  • Beautiful natural texture

Cons:

  • Heavy
  • Can chip if hit
  • Usually requires a waterproof sealant
  • Can leave alkaline residue if untreated
  • Not ideal if you frequently move décor around

Best style

  • Modern
  • Japandi
  • Scandinavian
  • Industrial
  • Minimalist

Wooden Vases

Why people choose wood:

  • Warm and natural
  • Unique grain patterns
  • Brings softness to modern spaces

Pros:

  • Lightweight
  • Warmest-looking material
  • Every piece is unique
  • Doesn't shatter if dropped
  • Great for dried flowers and branches

Cons:

  • Usually not waterproof
  • Can warp or crack with moisture
  • Often requires a glass insert for fresh flowers
  • Some woods fade in direct sunlight

Best style:

  • Organic modern
  • Japandi
  • Wabi-sabi
  • Mid-century modern
  • Nature-inspired décor
